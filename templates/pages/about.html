About DICHO Ltd

The Company Behind ALVI Natural Products

Who We Are

Company Overview

DICHO Ltd is a diversified manufacturing and trading company dedicated to delivering quality, natural, and value-added products for everyday use. Through ALVI, the company focuses on beauty care, hair care, home care, nutrition, and selected packaged products.

The company combines product development, packaging, customer education, and digital access to make ALVI products easier to discover and order.
