Who We Are

Company Overview

DICHO Ltd is a diversified manufacturing and trading company dedicated to delivering high-quality, natural, and value-added products for everyday life. Through its ALVI brand, the company provides products across beauty, personal care, hair care, home care, nutrition, and selected agricultural products.

Rooted in quality, innovation, sustainability, and customer well-being, DICHO Ltd combines traditional knowledge with modern production standards to serve households, professionals, retailers, wholesalers, salons, spas, hotels, restaurants, and distributors.
