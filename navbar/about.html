Who We Are

Company Overview

DICHO Ltd is a diversified manufacturing and trading company dedicated to delivering high-quality, natural and value-added products for everyday life.

Our portfolio spans cosmetics and personal care, hair care, edible and cooking oils, aromatherapy essential oils, liquid detergents, spices, nuts, dried products and selected fresh agricultural products.

Rooted in a strong commitment to quality, innovation, sustainability and customer well-being, DICHO Ltd combines traditional knowledge with modern production standards to serve households, professionals and retailers.

Our Vision

To become a trusted regional and international brand offering natural, effective and affordable products that enhance beauty, health, hygiene and nutrition.

Our Mission

  • Manufacture and distribute high-quality products using carefully selected raw materials.
  • Promote natural and plant-based solutions for personal care, food and home hygiene.
  • Create sustainable value for customers, partners, farmers and communities.
